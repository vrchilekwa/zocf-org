About the chapter

Who we are

The Women’s chapter brings together the women of ZOCF from every regional chapter. We meet to pray, to encourage one another in faith and family life, and to build lasting friendships.

Fellowship and prayer

Meeting together to pray, study the Bible and share life.

Conferences

Coming together at the ZOCF Women’s Conference.

Mentoring

Older and younger women supporting and learning from one another.

Caring for each other

Standing with one another through life’s seasons.
