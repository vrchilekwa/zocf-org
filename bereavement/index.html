Why it matters

Loss is harder far from home

One of the practical expressions of community living is the responsibility to support one another during difficult circumstances.

Bereavement is particularly challenging for diaspora communities. When a family loses a loved one, there can be significant emotional, logistical and financial pressures, particularly for families living far from their extended support networks. Families may need to arrange funeral services, transport and, where applicable, repatriation.

Being developed

A community bereavement and funeral support arrangement

Individual fundraising, including online fundraising, can help, but it may not always raise what is needed quickly enough. Insurance may also be unaffordable or unsuitable for some members.

ZOCF is therefore exploring a community-based arrangement built on mutual assistance and collective responsibility, in which participating members contribute an agreed amount in advance and the community assists a bereaved member or eligible family in accordance with agreed rules. It would seek to:

  • Reduce the financial burden associated with bereavement
  • Make assistance available more promptly
  • Encourage preparedness through collective participation
  • Strengthen relationships and trust among members
  • Provide a reliable community support mechanism
  • Promote dignity and compassion during difficult circumstances

This arrangement is not yet in place

No contributions are being collected and no payment or financial benefit is promised. The arrangement will need clear rules on membership, eligibility, contributions, payments, accountability and disputes, and ZOCF will take financial, legal and regulatory advice before anything is offered.
