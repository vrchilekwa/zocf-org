Events & Fellowship Schedule

From weekly prayer to our biggest celebration of the year — there’s always a seat for you.

  1. Every Week Virtual · Zoom

    Weekly Fellowship Prayer Call

    An hour of praise, intercession and the Word. Bring your prayer requests, your testimonies — and your family.

    When

    Thursdays · 19:00 UK / 21:00 Zambia

    Duration

    60 minutes

    Meeting ID

    000 0000 0000

    Passcode

    Shared on sign-up

    Join on Zoom
  2. Last Sunday Monthly

    Bible Study & Family Fellowship

    A relaxed, interactive study for all ages, followed by open sharing and prayer for families, students and newcomers. Held on Zoom.

  3. Annual · October Signature Event

    Zambia Independence Celebrations

    Each October we mark Independence Day (24 October) with a thanksgiving service, cultural performances, traditional food and fellowship — online and in person in host cities worldwide.

    🙏 Thanksgiving service 🥁 Music & dance 🍲 Nshima & home cooking
  4. Annual · December

    Christmas Carols & Year-End Thanksgiving

    Close the year in worship with carols in English and local languages, testimonies and a prayer of blessing over the year ahead.

Membership

You don’t have to do life abroad alone.

Become an official member by completing the form and paying the annual membership fee. We’ll send you the prayer call link, upcoming events and a personal welcome from someone in your region.

Member benefits

  • Weekly prayer call access and connection with Zambians near you
  • Regular newsletter updates on ZOCF-sponsored and supported activities — and a say in how they develop
  • Help organise, plan and take ownership of ZOCF conferences, seminars and symposia
  • Qualify for a leadership role at any level within ZOCF
