ZOCF at a glance

A member-led community organisation

Who we are

A UK registered charity (No. 1177269), started by Zambians in 2001, bringing together Zambian Christians in the diaspora. We are member-led and volunteer-driven.

What we do

We build community through stronger relationships, charitable works and productive engagement, working through regional and functional chapters.

Why it matters more widely

Isolation and weakening social connections affect the whole of society. Our community-living model promotes inclusion, volunteering, intergenerational relationships and cultural understanding.

Our approach to funding

Funding enables initiatives; it is not an end in itself

Projects should emerge from genuine needs and opportunities identified through community engagement. We seek to ensure that funded initiatives have:

1

Clearly defined objectives

2

Identifiable community needs

3

Active participation

4

Measurable benefits

5

Inclusion and accessibility

6

Responsible use of resources

7

Accountability and reporting

8

Sustainable community development

Restricted donations are used for their stated purposes, and all funds are managed through approved ZOCF financial procedures. Funding opportunities may include charitable grants, donations, sponsorship and appropriately structured fundraising activities.

Working in partnership

What partnership can provide

  • Specialist knowledge and training
  • Access to facilities
  • Volunteer opportunities
  • Joint programme delivery
  • Funding and material resources
  • Mentorship and professional support

We seek to contribute to established charitable activities rather than duplicate existing services, and to build partnerships that bring together different skills, experiences and resources.
