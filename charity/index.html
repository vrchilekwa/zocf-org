Our model

Every gift goes to those in need

Caring for the community in the UK

  • Run ZOCF NextGen. Our community for young adults aged 18–40, with regular gatherings, mentoring, peer support and opportunities to serve and lead.
  • Hold wellbeing check-ins and seminars. Safe spaces to talk about mental health, identity and living between cultures, led by qualified professionals and pastors, with signposting to further help where needed.
  • Strengthen cultural identity. Zambian language and heritage activities, and events such as Independence celebrations, so young people know and value where they come from.
  • Bring children and families together. Family days and youth events in our chapters, where younger children build friendships with others who share their background.
  • Keep young people connected between events. Through our website, Facebook, Instagram and virtual meetings, so that no one is left isolated because of where they live.

Looking ahead

  • Double youth and young adult gatherings
  • Increase wellbeing check-ins and seminars (speakers, materials)
  • Increase language and heritage activities
  • Double children’s and family events
  • Improve online platforms and virtual meetings
