Where we serve

Our charitable projects

From our communities in the UK to families back home in Zambia, these are some of the ways ZOCF puts faith into action.

  • Ongoing

    Back-to-School Support

    Zambia

    Providing uniforms, books and school fees for children from struggling families, so that no child misses out on education.

    Example project

  • December

    Christmas Hamper Drive

    United Kingdom

    Collecting and delivering food hampers and gifts to families, students and elderly members of our community who are facing a hard Christmas.

    Example project

  • Planned

    Clean Water for Rural Communities

    Zambia

    Raising funds for a borehole to give a rural village safe, reliable drinking water, and improving health for children and families.

    Example project

  • Ongoing

    Hardship & Bereavement Support

    United Kingdom

    Standing with members in difficult times, with prayer, pastoral care and practical help when families face loss, illness or hardship.

    Example project

  • Planned

    Medical Supplies Appeal

    Zambia

    Gathering essential medical supplies and equipment for rural clinics and hospitals, working with trusted partners on the ground.

    Example project

  • Ongoing

    Newcomer Welcome Packs

    UK & Ireland chapters

    Helping Zambians who are new to the UK and Ireland settle in, with welcome packs, local contacts and a church family from day one.

    Example project
