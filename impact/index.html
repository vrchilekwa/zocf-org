Proposed roadmap

Where we are heading

Phase 1: Establishing the foundations

First three months

  • Consult members and regional leadership on the framework
  • Identify a community initiatives coordinating team
  • Develop simple volunteer registration and proposal forms
  • Map existing skills, interests and activities
  • Identify two or three manageable pilot initiatives
  • Establish minimum safeguarding, financial and reporting procedures

Phase 2: Piloting community initiatives

Months four to six

  • Launch selected pilot projects in participating regions
  • Recruit and orient volunteers
  • Develop initial training and mentoring resources
  • Engage charitable and professional partners
  • Publish volunteering opportunities
  • Review early outcomes and challenges

Phase 3: Expanding and strengthening

Months seven to twelve

  • Extend successful initiatives to more regions
  • Strengthen coordination between regional and functional chapters
  • Develop the community knowledge and training toolkit
  • Introduce volunteer recognition and learning sessions
  • Seek funding for suitable programmes
  • Publish an initial community impact review

An indicative timetable

This roadmap is part of a first draft for consultation. It will depend on consultation, available resources and formal approval.
