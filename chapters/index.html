Start a chapter

No chapter near you? Help us start one.

If there are Zambians and friends of Zambia in your area who would like to meet for fellowship, tell us about it. Complete the request form and the ZOCF leadership will contact you to talk through the next steps.

What happens next

  1. 1Send your request. Tell us where you are and who is interested.
  2. 2We get in touch. A member of the leadership team contacts you to discuss your request.
  3. 3Your chapter is listed. Once agreed, your chapter gets its own section on this page for photos and videos.
