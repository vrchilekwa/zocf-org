Governance and accountability

Well governed and community-led

A grassroots organisation needs a governance structure that supports participation while providing accountability, direction and continuity.

How ZOCF is led

Three roles that work together

Governance: the Board of Trustees

Provides direction, oversight and accountability. The Board retains ultimate responsibility for ensuring that activities are consistent with ZOCF’s governing document, charitable purposes and legal obligations.

Coordination: national and regional leadership

Coordinates activities between regional and functional chapters, supports communication and keeps the organisation aligned with its vision.

Participation: steering committees

Volunteer members in each chapter who turn community needs and ideas into practical activities.

Safeguarding

Keeping children and adults at risk safe

Activities involving children or adults at risk require appropriate safeguarding arrangements, including training, risk assessment, supervision and relevant checks where legally required.

Volunteers who work with children or adults at risk will receive guidance on safeguarding, confidentiality and the boundaries of their role. Volunteers are not expected to provide regulated medical, counselling or other professional services unless appropriately qualified, authorised and covered by suitable arrangements.

The name and contact details of the ZOCF safeguarding lead will be published here.

If you believe someone is in immediate danger, call 999.

Accountability

Responsible use of what is entrusted to us

Financial controls

All project funds, donations and expenditure must be recorded and managed through approved ZOCF financial procedures. Restricted donations must be used for their stated purposes.

Volunteer wellbeing

Volunteers receive clear expectations, appropriate support and a safe environment in which to contribute. No member should feel pressured to volunteer beyond their capacity.

Reporting

Initiatives are monitored and reviewed, with reporting kept proportionate to their size and complexity.

Complaints and concerns

How to raise a concern

Volunteers, members and beneficiaries can raise concerns, including safeguarding issues, financial irregularities or inappropriate conduct. Concerns are passed to the appropriate member of the leadership or the Board of Trustees.

Data protection

How we use your information

Personal information relating to members, volunteers and beneficiaries is handled securely, used for legitimate purposes and shared only where appropriate.

What we collect
The details you give us on our forms: your name, email address, phone number, chapter, and anything you tell us in a membership registration, volunteer registration, initiative proposal or request to start a chapter.
Why we collect it
To respond to you, to manage your membership, to match volunteers with opportunities, and to consider the proposals and requests you send us.
Who sees it
Members of the ZOCF leadership who need it to respond to you. Our online forms are delivered by Formspree, a third-party form service, which sends your submission to ZOCF by email. We do not sell your information or share it for marketing.
How long we keep it
Only for as long as it is needed for the purpose you gave it, or as long as the law requires.
Your choices
You can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw your consent to be contacted, by emailing contact@zocf.org. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).
