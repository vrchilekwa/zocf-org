About the chapter

Who we are

The Men’s chapter brings together the men of ZOCF from every regional chapter. We meet to pray, to encourage one another in faith, family and work, and to build lasting friendships.

Fellowship and prayer

Meeting together to pray, study the Bible and share life.

Accountability

Men encouraging one another to live out their faith.

Mentoring

Older and younger men supporting and learning from one another.

Serving together

Putting faith into practice in our families and community.
